How it works

From measurement to running workloads in four steps

  1. 01

    Measure your supply

    Start from real generation, tariff and site data rather than an assumed best case.

  2. 02

    Size the node

    Use the planner to match load, runtime and storage to the share of solar you can allocate.

  3. 03

    Commission safely

    Energy work is designed and signed off by qualified professionals; you build and own the compute.

  4. 04

    Operate on policy

    Schedule workloads by urgency and generation, then review measured results honestly.
