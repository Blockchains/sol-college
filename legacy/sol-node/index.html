What ships with a node

Documentation is part of the product

A node you cannot explain to an installer, an auditor or a finance team is not deployable.

  • Measured power profile at idle, burst and sustained load
  • Thermal, noise and siting guidance with ambient limits
  • Read-only telemetry integration for site generation data
  • Update, rollback and incident runbook
  • Monthly energy and compute reporting template

Sol Node covers compute hardware and software only. Photovoltaic, battery and electrical installation work must be carried out by appropriately qualified and certified professionals under local regulations.
