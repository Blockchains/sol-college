Flagship course

The Solar AI Node Blueprint

Eight modules taking you from energy fundamentals to a written pilot plan with a defined baseline.

  1. 01

    Energy, storage and compute fundamentals

    How generation, storage and load interact over a day and a year, and the vocabulary used across the rest of the course.

  2. 02

    Understanding AI-node power demand

    Idle draw, burst draw and sustained load; measuring at the socket; turning measurements into an annual demand estimate.

  3. 03

    Solar-first versus always-on workloads

    Classifying workloads by urgency and interruptibility, and deciding deliberately which ones are always-on.

  4. 04

    Battery, grid and cloud fallback

    Reserve floors, evening load competition, grid supply as the reliability backbone, and cloud burst for peaks.

  5. 05

    Telemetry and energy data

    Sources, intervals, sign conventions, normalisation and safe handling of stale or missing data.

  6. 06

    Local AI deployment

    Model selection, quantisation, serving, storage layout and concurrency limits appropriate to your node tier.

  7. 07

    Safety, commissioning and operations

    Competence boundaries, information to prepare for installers, thermal and noise planning, monitoring and incident handling.

  8. 08

    Designing your pilot

    Scoping a single workload, defining a baseline, choosing metrics and writing a report that states its own limitations.
