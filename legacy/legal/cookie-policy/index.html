What we use

Sign-in storage: when you create an account, your browser stores a session so you stay signed in between visits. Clearing it signs you out.

Preferences: small values such as filters you have chosen, stored in your browser only.

What we do not use

We do not run advertising cookies, cross-site trackers or third-party marketing pixels, and we do not sell behavioural data.

Managing storage

You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. The site still works signed out; you simply cannot see saved plans or reading.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or embedded media that sets cookies, we will list it here and ask for consent where required.
