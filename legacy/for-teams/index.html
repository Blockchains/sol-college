Who we work with

  • Workload classification workshop
  • Node sizing and supply-path review
  • Scheduling policy design

How an engagement runs

Five stages, no surprises

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Forty-five minutes on your site, workloads and constraints. We say clearly if this is not the right fit.

  2. 02

    Baseline

    Measure existing generation, consumption and workload demand before changing anything.

  3. 03

    Design review

    Node tier, supply path, monitoring plan and the professional commissioning route for any electrical work.

  4. 04

    Pilot

    Six weeks of operation with weekly reviews against the baseline.

  5. 05

    Report and handover

    An honest written report, operator training and a decision about what to do next.
